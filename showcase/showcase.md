# Fathom

The trench is dark. You are a small, cold light with a very large appetite. Thread flooded passages and graze their glowing plankton, revealing a little more of the maze with every mouthful. Eat everything, survive the descent, and find out how deep you can go.

In the Kindle dive, the world lives inside a circle of light that grows as you feed. Explored paths linger in memory, then disappear beyond its edge. Send a cyan sonar crest around a blind corner to see what waits there. The answer may hear you asking.

Three hunters share the water. A Lanternjaw drifts beneath an amber bell, indistinguishable from a harmless meal until its jaws come out. An eyeless Gloamfin follows sound, gaining on straightaways and losing speed around corners. A Flarefish charges a sudden bloom that sees through the stone itself. Watch their tells, break their pursuit, and leave ink in your wake.

Each cleared maze takes you deeper, into more hunters and a shorter sonar reach. Your light is a way forward and a signal to everything hungry. Keep moving. Keep listening. Not every glimmer is yours to eat.
